Privacy Policy for Zombie Road

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zombie Road handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Zombie Road does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

The game stores certain gameplay settings and progress locally on your device. This may include selected vehicle or zombie skins, game settings, coins, and distance records.

2. Local Storage

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device so that your settings and progress can be restored when you open the game again. This data is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

3. Internet and Online Services

The current version of Zombie Road does not require an online account or our own server to play the game. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay data.

4. Advertising

The current version described by this policy does not include an advertising SDK or personalized advertising service.

If advertising or another third-party service is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that version is released to explain what information the service may collect and how it is used.

5. Analytics

The current version does not use a third-party analytics service to collect personal or gameplay analytics from users.

6. Children's Privacy

Zombie Road does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because the game does not require an account and does not directly request personal information, users can play without providing such information.

7. Data Security

Since the game does not maintain a user account or transmit gameplay data to our own servers, there is no user account database maintained by us for this game. Locally stored game data remains subject to the security of the user's device.

8. Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling Zombie Road from your device, subject to the operating system's behavior.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally share personal information with third-party service providers. If third-party services are added later, this policy will be updated to identify their relevant data practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its features, or its data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same location with a new “Last updated” date.

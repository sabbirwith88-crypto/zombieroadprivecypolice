This Privacy Policy explains how Zombie Road Extra (“Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), published by mdalamin97, handles information when you install or use the Game.

1. About the Game

Zombie Road Extra is a mobile action game. The Game does not require users to create an account or sign in. Gameplay can be used without providing us with a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or messages.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device so that the Game can remember your progress and settings. This may include:

  • Coins and other in-game progress;
  • Best distance or score information;
  • Owned and selected cars, roads, characters/skins, or similar game items;
  • Game settings, including sound, vibration, and control preferences;
  • Other non-account gameplay state needed to operate the Game.

This information is stored locally by the Game and is not an online user account. If you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, locally stored gameplay information may be removed by Android.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. The Game may show interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with the device or advertising service in order to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertising services. Depending on the device, region, consent status, and applicable settings, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifier or other advertising-related identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from an IP address;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, and technical information;
  • Information about advertisements that are shown, viewed, clicked, or otherwise interacted with;
  • Information used for fraud prevention, security, measurement, and advertising delivery.

We do not independently sell your personal information to advertisers. Information processed by Google or other advertising partners is subject to their own privacy policies and applicable controls.

The Game may offer optional in-game rewards after you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement. For example, a rewarded advertisement may be offered for in-game coins or an opportunity to continue gameplay.

This section describes the Game's direct behavior. Third-party services, including advertising services, may independently process technical or advertising-related information as described above.
